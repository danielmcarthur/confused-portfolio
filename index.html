About

I've spent about five years in data and AI consulting, helping organisations work out what to build and how to make it useful.

Before that, I was big into Marketing, copywriting, graphic design and some limited digital marketing in the early days.

In 2026, I have taken some purposeful steps to tap back into my creative side and see what I can produce in protest to AI.

I see the intersection of technology, data and art - and I want to keep pulling on that thread.
